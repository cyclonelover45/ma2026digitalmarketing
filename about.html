Meet the Outfitter

"My name is Max Anderson and I am an Outfitter from Northern Minnesota. I've lived in MN all my life, and have no plans of going anywhere. Fishing and golf enthusiast."

Primary Skills & Specialties

With years of experience navigating northern waterways, specialization includes:

  • Trout Fishing: Stream and river tactics for wild and stocked trout species.
  • Northern Pike: Targeting trophy pike across wilderness lakes and river systems.
  • Smallmouth & Largemouth Bass: Kayak and boat positioning for bass in clear waters and structure.
